# How Many Shawarmas? 🌯

Everything costs shawarmas. Type any price or item and see what it really costs.

## What it does

- **Convert anything**: type a price (`499`, `50k`, `3b`) or an item name (`iphone 11`, `lambo`, typos are fine).
- **10+ silly units**: shawarmas, tacos, Uber rides, hours of minimum wage, Netflix months...
- **Daily challenge**: the same 5 items for everyone every day. Guess the price in shawarmas, score out of 500, keep your streak.
- **Practice mode**: random rounds, as many as you want.
- **Price list**: 600+ items in 19 categories (phones, sneakers, cars, homes, cities, US states, countries, rockets, food and more), searchable.
- **Share cards**: story-sized images (1080×1920) for TikTok / Instagram.

## Run it

It's one file. Open `index.html` in a browser. No install, no build.

## Prices

Prices are rough US prices (checked October 2026). 1 shawarma = $10.
To change a price, edit the `QUESTIONS` or `EXTRA_ITEMS` lists in `index.html`.

## Hosting

Works on GitHub Pages or Netlify as-is.
